Is this backtest evidence, or the best of many coin flips?

An AI agent proposes trading strategies. An auditor tries to reject them.

A research lab that counts every strategy ever tried and passes only results that survive deflated Sharpe, sub-period stability and alpha over a 60/40 portfolio.

1.17→ −0.37

Sharpe of the one strategy that passed, in research and then on locked holdout data. I logged the prediction that it would fail before opening the holdout.

Situation
Try enough ideas and the best backtest looks impressive by luck alone. An allocator or risk team has to know whether a result is evidence or the winner of a large search.
What I did
Built a backtest engine where strategies are JSON specs, never code, an auditor with three tests, and an append-only ledger so no tried idea can be hidden. An LLM agent proposes strategies under a fixed trial budget and sees research data only.
Outcome
Across 500 simulated searches, the best of 200 pure-noise strategies passed a naive test every time and the auditor never. No textbook strategy passed. When the agent was also shown past returns, it built portfolios from the in-sample winners, and that is the only way a strategy got through.
The auditor
Deflated Sharpe ≥ 0.95 given every trial · positive in at least 5 of 6 sub-periods · deflated alpha over 60/40 ≥ 0.95 · probability of backtest overfitting (CSCV) reported for each search
Guardrails
One timing rule tested by corrupting future data · every leak test checked by planting the bug it should catch · 10 bps costs, cash at T-bills · 2022 onward locked as a holdout

The agent writes specs, not code, so it cannot leak future data into a result. It sees assets as letters and periods as numbers, which keeps a model trained through 2024 from using what it already knows about 2022. An information ablation across three seeds showed what it does with more: shown each asset’s past returns, it picked the winners, a search the ledger never counted.

One strategy over 56 months in one rate regime is consistent with the snooping explanation, not proof of it. The lasting change is in the design: the agent now sees risk statistics only, and past returns reach it only through counted trials.
