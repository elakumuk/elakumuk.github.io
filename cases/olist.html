How delivery delays relate to customer reviews

A logistics problem showing up in customer reviews

I analyzed delivery performance and customer segments to identify where an operations team could focus its retention and service work.

Situation
Olist’s public marketplace dataset contains nearly 100,000 orders, customer records, delivery dates, payments, and reviews. The business question: which customer and delivery patterns deserve attention?
What I did
Built SQL analyses and a pandas workflow for delivery performance, review scores, sales trends, and RFM segmentation. I verified that customer_id is an order-level key and used customer_unique_id for person-level repeat-purchase analysis.
What the data shows
Among reviewed delivered orders, late deliveries averaged 2.57/5 versus 4.29/5 for on-time or early deliveries. This is an association in observational data, not proof that lateness alone caused the lower ratings.

Business recommendations
Prioritize investigating the longest-delay lanes, where observed review scores are lowest. Treat the 97% one-time-buyer rate as a retention opportunity, then validate a win-back intervention with a controlled test before scaling it.
Limits
This is a historical public dataset from 2016–2018. The review comparisons are descriptive; the analysis does not isolate delivery delay from product, seller, geography, or customer differences.
