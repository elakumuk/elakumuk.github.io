What the proposed study would test

The proposed study would randomize participants across two independent factors: how often performance is shown and how often an allocation can be changed. This demo illustrates the flow only; it is not a study and cannot test a hypothesis. Historical data source: Kenneth French Data Library (monthly Fama/French factors; 202608 archive vintage).
